Status: source acceptance passed; committed-source packaging and signed

## Changes

- Add Modeling **Straighten** and **Restore shape**, shared create/edit dialogs
  and six console commands. Supported additive solid Revolutions and 2D/3D/H
  Sweeps retain their constant cross-section; developed length follows the filled
  section centroid trajectory and a positive length coefficient.
- Carry supported tangent end-section continuation chains through both states,
  including finite Extrusions and curved Revolutions/Sweeps. Retain authored
  placements, exact reference sides, native reference packets, Undo/Redo and
  Family variants. Preserve supported Fillets and holes wholly in straight
  portions. Unsupported joins, variable sections and cuts crossing curved
  portions fail without partially committing a model.
- Keep Drawing dimensions, sections, details, crops, breaks and vector exports
  attached to regenerated state geometry without recalculating the source Part.
- Preserve full loaded placement coordinates and angles independently of display
  rounding. Opening and accepting unchanged attached Properties creates no
  model change. Actual numerical edits still replace the stored exact value.
- Restore circular/elliptical axes for general Feature Extrusion sides and expose
  profile, origin and centroid axes in the Tree. Whole-feature View selection
  highlights their lines as well as endpoints, without highlighting another
  feature or repeated occurrence. Existing standalone reference keys remain.
- Initialize Origin display scale from datum geometry even when the preceding
  scene contained only a point or body and its camera is preserved.
- Include Czech, English, German, French and Russian UI translations and updated
  English user/console documentation. Boundary Surface redesign, File Manager
  and extended Drawing captions remain queued separately.
