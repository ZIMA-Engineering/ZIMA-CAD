# ZIMA-CAD 2026112202

FEM linear statics now solves pressure on Body faces and bounded planar or
projected Sketch regions. Displacement and equivalent von Mises stress have
switchable color maps with unit-labelled legends in the right-hand Results menu.
Stress is averaged within each tetrahedron without smoothing across elements.

Quadratic meshes are checked for inverted elements after curving onto CAD.
An invalid mesh is optimized against its CAD geometry and checked again before
it can be used. A saved twisted-profile case now meshes and solves successfully.

Mesh and Calculation commands are in the right FEM toolbar. Selecting an owned
Sketch plane highlights its resolved XY/XZ/YZ or referenced plane in azure,
including its offset.

The [pressure verification](../verification/20261122-fem-pressure.md) and
[mesh/result verification](../verification/20261122-fem-results.md) record the
finite acceptance scope. This remains small-displacement isotropic linear
elasticity. Thermal analysis, contact, plasticity and dynamics are not included.
Linux execution remains unverified in this Windows release cycle.
