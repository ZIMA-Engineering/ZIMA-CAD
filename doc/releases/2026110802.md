# Windows 2026110802 — Curved Fillet and Chamfer termination

Status: Windows source candidate pending.

ZIMA Fillet and Chamfer now share the same automatic route-end contract. A
selected treatment route can reach the exact curved envelope of the current
Body, including cylindrical terminal faces, instead of stopping at the route
vertex or extending a construction tool through the Body. The builder extends
only its internal tool by a bounded amount; the final CUT or FUSE establishes
the exact Body boundary. Explicit Up To faces remain authoritative.

The common terminal resolver distinguishes three cases: an earlier treatment
which is the local stop, a compound earlier radius which must continue to its
first adjoining planar face, and a curved Body envelope crossed by the selected
route. This preserves the working local-radius behavior while fixing the
reported `12.prtz` Fillet 03 cylinder termination and the corresponding
Chamfer case. The same rule is used for additive and subtractive construction
with their correct material sense.

Custom ZIMA Chamfer now consumes the exact persisted route membership instead
of allowing OCCT fallback to expand one remaining segment to a complete tangent
contour. This fixes the reported `11.prtz` Chamfer 02 case after continuation
segments are removed. Fillet and Chamfer also share accepted-result ancestry,
validation and exact-topology preservation, reducing behavioral drift between
the two commands without duplicating the modeling algorithm.

The lightweight azure Fillet preview prefers calculated support-face sections
for rolling plane/circle, circle/circle, ellipse/circle and spline/circle
contacts. It therefore shows the real treatment boundary instead of the former
tangent-line cage. Ordinary selection remains wire-only and performs no OCCT
calculation.

Deleting a selected or dimension-inspected history treatment now retires its
View and dimension handles before the document transaction releases calculated
topology. Undo restores the treatment and ordinary selection remains usable.
This closes the reported intermittent crash and loss of azure selection after
deleting a Fillet or Chamfer.

The focused native, document and GUI regression matrix passed, including exact
Fillet and Chamfer builders, curved additive/subtractive terminals, two- and
three-branch nodes, direct batch calculation, route queries, lightweight wires,
history deletion and all five localization catalogs. No user-visible text was
added or changed. The broader historical suite was sampled separately and is
not claimed completely green; its unrelated existing Sketcher, browser, legacy
command and sandbox-dependent failures are outside this release scope.

The native file format, start templates, factory library, shared placement
contract, geometry-side contract, geometry precision and Qt/OCCT 8.0.1 pins are
unchanged. Native Linux compilation and execution remain a separate host gate.
