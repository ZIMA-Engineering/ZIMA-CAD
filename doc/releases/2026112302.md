# ZIMA-CAD 2026112302

## Modeling

- Circles and arcs can be tangent to noncircular Offsets, including tested
  ellipse and spline sources. Contacts retain their real native point and
  independent C constraints through repeated dimensions, dragging and reopening.
- Straighten carries supported dependent features and their cutting limits into
  the transformed frame. The supplied twisted Part straightens and restores on
  an isolated copy. An unchanged second OK can retain a failed definition without
  repeating the failed calculation.
- Scale, Mirror and Pattern keep their axis endpoints available for picking and
  references without permanent black point markers, including nested operations.

## Render and FEM

- Camera and light objects remain visible and selectable. View through camera is
  available in Properties and context menus, with projection and field of view
  retained when switching document tabs.
- The image tab uses the standard right command column, pointer-anchored wheel
  zoom and Drawing-style panning. Rename and Delete are image Tree context
  actions. Export belongs to the image tab; Save to Part retains the image.
- FEM and Render marks and adjacent text use dimension yellow. Planar and
  projected FEM Sketch region boundaries follow the same convention, while
  ordinary hover/selection and authored Sketch editing retain their interactions.

See the [finite verification matrix](../verification/20261123-offset-render-straighten.md)
for tested combinations and remaining limits. Arbitrary nested nonlinear Offsets
and all possible Straighten attachments are not claimed. CAM and image cropping
remain separate pending work. Native file schemas and dependency pins are unchanged.

## Distribution status
