# ZIMA-CAD 2026112205

FEM Body properties now offer **Thermal-mechanical analysis**. A single load
case combines steady-state heating, thermal expansion, forces, pressure and
mechanical restraints. Results show temperature, displacement and von Mises
stress. Measurement points report temperature and combined displacement.

The separate **Distance measurement** command references two existing measurement
points in the same FEM Body. It reports their original distance, deformed
distance and signed difference, independent of visual magnification or animation.
References can be entered through the Tree or View. Missing references keep
their last valid positions and remain repairable.

Meshing now handles coincident, overlapping and disjoint projected heat/pressure
patches on the same face without letting one partition invalidate another.

Sketcher can add a second circular tangency and keep both tangencies editable
after trimming to an arc. Radius/diameter edits and repeated point dragging
preserve native contact points and read-only projected supports.

See the [combined FEM workflow](../FEM_COMBINED_AND_DISTANCE.md),
[FEM verification](../verification/20261122-fem-combined.md) and
[Sketcher verification](../verification/20261122-double-circular-tangency.md).
Thermomechanics is one-way, steady-state, small-strain analysis with constant
isotropic material properties. Native Linux execution remains unverified.
