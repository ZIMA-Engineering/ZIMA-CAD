# ZIMA-CAD 2026092605 — Windows

This build adds tilted sheet transitions and rectangular L/U transitions.

- Half-round transitions support two-axis tilt through planar triangulation
  where the common-tangent construction is unavailable.
- Rectangular transitions connect two rectangular profiles with two adjacent
  walls (L) or three walls (U), including relative three-axis rotation.
- Converging bend tips receive a straight, through-thickness relief. Small
  gaps are intentional weld preparation accepted by the user.
- The rectangular toolbar and history icons have a straight upper rim.
- Unbend preserves one connected solid and retries failed compound-junction
  fitting against the finer source budget. The document limit remains 0.05 mm.

The existing owned Sketch records store the profile mode and side count.
No native document schema change is introduced. The development entry point
remains `zima-cad.bat`. Command text is localized in Czech, English, German,
French and Russian.

Solid Straighten/Restore shape commands remain separate pending work and are
not included in this release. Linux is not part of this Windows acceptance.

## Acceptance

All ten focused transition, sheet-state, cone, Twisted Sheet, native command,
GUI and translation contracts passed after the apex-relief changes (184.06 s
total). The transition geometry suite took 112.77 s and the native transition
transaction suite 85.23 s. The checks include one connected solid, constant
thickness, relief-edge development, unique panel edge roles, volume budgets,
cold reopen, Undo/Redo and restored material. The rectangular icon and model
were visually inspected in the application. Packaging unit checks passed
(nine cases, two platform-specific skips).

The follow-up [coverage review](../TRANSITION_COVERAGE.md) records missing
complete/seamed, eccentric round and general-profile transitions.
