# ZIMA-CAD 2026101701

The engineering library contains 222 editable hexagon-head bolts and screws
across ISO 4014, 4016, 4017, 4018, 8676, 8765 and historical CSN 02 1201,
1205, 1207 and 1208. Each listed diameter/pitch/product-grade combination has
one suitable representative length, five-language metadata and an individual
native thumbnail. Partial threads and runouts stop before the underhead fillet.
Grade C omits the raised bearing face. Historical locking-hole variants have
real transverse holes centered in the head. ISO tips follow the reviewed
chamfered-end limits. Product grades remain separate from material strength class.

Models are placed directly in their table chapters. Ten independent native
reference Parts/Drawings and dimensioned illustrations accompany the standards.
The original four small ISO 4017 filenames remain stable; stock follows actual
dimensions through Relations. Save As retains its established independent-copy
behavior and leaves the original active.

Drawing dimensions can pick the actual sloping head boundaries and edge-on
circular references. Meaningful geometry contacts replace arbitrary curve-point
picking. Thick radius contours remain above thin strokes, closed circular rims
retain their ends, and outside linear/chain dimensions retain clearance when
their source geometry changes.

Sketch Origin snapping takes priority over a single axis and creates the actual
Origin contact. Construction and auxiliary wires use the established green;
their native points use the ordinary theme foreground. The construction toolbar
icon follows the same green. The thread dialog omits its unnecessary Origin
button, and the repaired factory Dimpled Hole retains centered native contacts.

Engineering HTML aligns chapter title, language links and company logo in one
row. The ZIMA logo preserves corporate green/white and changes its dark ink with
the theme, with transparent surroundings. Native automatic overviews recognize
previously imported copies of the artwork and update on live theme changes.
