## Behavior

- General Surface owns ordinary Sketches and 3D Curves inside its history
  container. Nested edits share its pending transaction, and the command consumes
  the existing complete placement contract without changing the shared solver.
- Fill Surface replaces the previous Boundary Surface name and preserves its
  reference workflow. Closed two-curve, triangular and general contours support
  positional G0 and explicitly supported G1/G2 boundary conditions.
- Sewing creates connected manifold surface shells; the existing Fillet command
  operates on their actual input edges. Surface Intersection creates reusable
  bounded native curves and isolated contact points.
- Surface Trim retains the selected fragment of a calculated surface, including
  closed-loop interiors or exteriors with holes. Persisted boundary ancestry
  follows source movement and rejects a disappeared region. Unselected geometry
  remains present. Selection and inspection read calculated viewer data.
- Suppressing a feature suppresses its dependent history. Restoration enables
  the selected feature and its earlier prerequisites; later dependents remain
  suppressed until explicitly restored.
- Straighten/Restore preserve state geometry, original references and axes
  through repeated operations, including combined Revolution features. Axis
  endpoint markers use their geometry domain colour.
- Sheet Cut starting on one Flat skin and ending Up To the opposite skin works
  in both directions, including clearance and rotated Body cases.
- The native `sheetm/ZE-BEND-NOTE.symz` symbol provides a manually authored bend
  note for drawings. The author sets angle and direction for the actual view.

Grid editing and G3 are deferred. Ambiguous native parent identities and
incompatible boundary conditions produce explicit calculation errors. Native
format compatibility with older documents is not promised.

B-spline preservation, closed-loop trimming, mixed solids, source translations of
500 mm, missing-region rejection, native reopening and dependency repair.
Seven focused surface/skin/dialog/translation contracts and the broad shared UI
contract passed. Real GUI checks cover rollback, common picking, reference
inspection, unchanged OK, Cancel and Undo/Redo. Every new UI/error string has
Czech, English, German, French and Russian translations.

Factory Part, Skeleton and Assembly templates were rewritten and reopened with
the current serializer; their tracked bytes are unchanged. The entire local
Windows build completed. Package tooling passed ten tests with two environment
skips; publisher tooling passed all sixteen tests. Full serial regression and
fresh committed-source packaging are the remaining acceptance gates.

The stable repository `zima-cad.bat` launches the current local C++ build.

the source commit and release. Linux acceptance remains a separate host task.
