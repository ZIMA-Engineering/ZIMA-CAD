SymbolMeaning
ISO/R 775:1969Cylindrical and 1:10 conical ends; withdrawn, with no direct current ISO replacement established.

Original scope excludes combustion engines, marine shafts and controls. From d = 220 mm the keyway follows the taper generatrix. Internal threads refer to ČSN 01 4917, reliefs to ČSN 01 4960; current national-reference status is unverified.
