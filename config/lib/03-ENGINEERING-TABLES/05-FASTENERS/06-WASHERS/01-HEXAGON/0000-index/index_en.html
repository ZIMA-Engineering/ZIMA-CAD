Lengths in mm; other units are identified in the headers. Superscripts identify original notes.

Values separated by / retain the header symbol order. They are never automatically added.

Parentheses retain original non-preferred sizes. A dash is missing data; + denotes an originally offered combination, not a positive deviation.

The main tables and models use dimensions from the listed 2000 ISO editions. Historic CSN sizes are not merely renamed; sizes outside the ISO range are listed separately.

The ISO 7090 model has an upper outside chamfer at 45° and depth h/3; the standard permits 30° to 45° and depth h/4 to h/2.
