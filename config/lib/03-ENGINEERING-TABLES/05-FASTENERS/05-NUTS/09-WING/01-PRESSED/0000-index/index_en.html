Six native M3–M10 models. Historical CSN 02 1665 dimensions are compared with the STN 02 1665 excerpt. R1 is the wing arc and R2 the hub arc, called R5 in the archive. Here h is overall height and p hub height. The upper wing angle omitted by the table is selected as 70°; the lower root transition is tangent and follows the symmetric M10 pattern. This is not a complete manufacturing specification. Threads are schematic. Mass is calculated from geometry at steel density 7,850 kg/m³, not copied from indicative weights. Cast M12–M24 sizes remain separate unfinished studies.
