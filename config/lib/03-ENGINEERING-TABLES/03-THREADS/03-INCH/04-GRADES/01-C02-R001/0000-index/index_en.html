ISO inch thread accuracy classes · 1–3
ClassMeaning
1A / 1BLarge assembly allowance; the original source limits applicability to UNC/UNF from 0.25 in.
2A / 2BGeneral use. For external 2A threads distinguish allowance, tolerance and coating.
3A / 3BClose fitting; pitch and flank angle also matter.

Inch threads use different designations and profile requirements from metric threads. P = 25.4/TPI [mm]. UN/UNR is not Whitworth or pipe threading.
