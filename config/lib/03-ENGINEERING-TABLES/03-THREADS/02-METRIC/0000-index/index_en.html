ISO metric threads
SymbolMeaning
M12×1.5M12×1.5 denotes 12 mm diameter and 1.5 mm pitch; M12×1.5-6H/6g distinguishes internal and external tolerance classes. LH denotes left-hand threads. Distinguish lead and pitch for multiple-start threads; M16×Ph3P1.5 has two starts.
