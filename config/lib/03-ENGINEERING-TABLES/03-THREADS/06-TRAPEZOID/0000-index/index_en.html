Tr has a 30° profile. Multiple-start lead is Ph = nP; do not interchange Tr with 29° Acme. Profile dimensions and tolerance classes are covered by different parts of ISO 2901 through ISO 2904.

Uppercase D denotes internal threads, lowercase d external threads. P is pitch; multiple-start lead is Ph = nP. Numeric entries are not automatically the limits of an actual product.
