Sheet metal

Form

Insert a native library Form into sheet material.

Command in the app

Before you begin

Prepare calculated sheet faces and an available native Form definition.

Workflow

  1. Choose the Form definition from the library.
  2. Select the required sheet face or corner faces and locate the insertion with placement references.
  3. Inspect the formed preview and confirm OK.

Result

The Part stores its own inserted definition and forming operation.

Limits and cancellation

Form describes shape and manufacturing intent, not material-flow simulation; other library copies remain independent.
