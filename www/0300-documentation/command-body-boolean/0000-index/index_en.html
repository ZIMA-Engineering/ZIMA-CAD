Bodies and derived results

Boolean

Combine complete calculated Body results.

Command in the app

Before you begin

At Part root, prepare at least two available preceding Bodies.

Workflow

  1. Select the input Bodies.
  2. Choose union, difference or intersection; check operand order for difference.
  3. Confirm OK and inspect the result.

Result

A Part-level Boolean result is added; source histories remain editable.

Limits and cancellation

Only Bodies available before the insertion position can be operands.
