Assembly

Before you start

Prepare native Parts or subassemblies, then create an Assembly (.asmz). Each inserted occurrence has its own identity and placement.

Steps

  1. Use Insert component to add a Part or nested Assembly. Review its occurrence in the Tree.
  2. Open the occurrence's Properties. Arm a component reference field, select its geometry, then select the corresponding owning-Assembly reference.
  3. Choose the offered mate and value. Add independent references until the intended translational and rotational freedoms remain.
  4. Inspect the placement and confirm OK. Cancel discards pending changes and restores the prior placements, including dependent components.
  5. Use Active to edit a component's source. A Part exposes Modeling tools; a subassembly exposes its own Assembly tools. Use Return to assembly to return.

What to check

Only the immediate owning Assembly positions a component. Activate a subassembly before editing its internal placements. The top-level scene stays visible as context, and external geometry is read-only reference material.

Current calculated Part geometry appears in its occurrences. Regenerate explicitly solves mates and Assembly-owned operations; switching tabs does not. New material features are authored in Parts, not at Assembly level.
