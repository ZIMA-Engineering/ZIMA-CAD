Surfaces

Sew surfaces

Join calculated surface faces into one connected shell.

Command in the app

Before you begin

Prepare compatible free surfaces in the active editable Body.

Workflow

  1. Activate a reference row and select a face.
  2. Add the other faces and inspect their shared boundaries independently.
  3. Confirm OK and inspect the sewn shell.

Result

Selected surfaces form one connected manifold shell with preserved geometry.

Limits and cancellation

Sewing neither caps openings nor creates solid material; disconnected or nonmanifold results are rejected.
