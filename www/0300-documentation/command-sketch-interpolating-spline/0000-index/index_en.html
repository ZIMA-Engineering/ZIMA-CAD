Sketch

Interpolating spline

Create a smooth curve through confirmed points.

Command in the app

Before you begin

Open an editable Sketch and close any other Properties window. Work in its active plane.

Workflow

  1. Enter points on the curve with the left button.
  2. Check the preview and point order.
  3. Double-click the middle button to finish at the last confirmed point.

Result

The interpolating spline passes through the points; the completion cursor adds no extra point.

Limits and cancellation

Escape cancels unfinished construction. Check completed geometry with dimensions and dragging; the profile must suit its consuming operation.
